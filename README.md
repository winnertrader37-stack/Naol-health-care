# 🩺 Naol Health Scanner 3D

Welcome to **Naol Health Scanner 3D**, a modern, mobile-friendly wellness website designed to help users track their daily health habits and well-being.

## ✨ Features

- 📷 **Camera Access:** Open your camera and capture photos.
- 🖼️ **Photo Gallery:** View and download captured photos.
- 🧍 **3D Human Viewer:** Explore an illustrative 3D human model.
- 💚 **Wellness Assessment:** Record your energy, sleep, mood, activity, and water intake.
- 🌬️ **Breathing Exercise:** Follow guided breathing exercises.
- 💧 **Hydration Tracker:** Track daily water consumption and set a water goal.
- 📔 **Mood and Sleep Diary:** Record your mood, sleep hours, and personal notes.
- 📊 **Reports:** Generate and download wellness summaries.
- 🌙 **Dark and Light Themes:** Switch between visual themes.
- 📱 **Responsive Design:** Designed for mobile phones, tablets, and computers.
- 💾 **Local Storage:** Save supported information in your browser.

## 🛠️ Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Browser Camera API
- Local Storage
- Three.js for 3D visualization
